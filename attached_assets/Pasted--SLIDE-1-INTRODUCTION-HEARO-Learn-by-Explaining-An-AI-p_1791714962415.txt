# SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM

## Reading Doesn't Mean Understanding.

Students often believe they understand a topic after reading or watching an explanation.

But when asked to explain it themselves, they struggle.

**The Challenge:** How can students discover what they truly understand and what they still need to learn?

---

# SLIDE 3 — OUR SOLUTION

## Meet HEARO — AI That Listens.

Hearo transforms passive studying into active learning.

- Explain a topic using your voice or text.
- Get your ideas checked against trusted learning material.
- Discover mistakes and missing concepts.
- Receive feedback supported by source references.

**You explain. Hearo listens.**

---

# SLIDE 4 — HOW IT WORKS

## From Explanation to Understanding.

**01 — SELECT**

Choose a study topic and reference material.

**02 — EXPLAIN**

Speak or type in Arabic or English.

**03 — VERIFY**

AI checks your explanation against the source.

**04 — VISUALIZE**

A concept map highlights correct, incorrect, and missing ideas.

**05 — IMPROVE**

Review corrections and download your learning report.

# SLIDE 5 — PRODUCT DEMO

## See HEARO in Action.

**Live Transcript**
See the words as the learner explains.

**AI-Based Checking**
Compare explanations with the approved source when live checking is available.

**Visual Feedback**

- 🟢 Green — Supported
- 🟡 Yellow — Incomplete / Needs More Detail
- 🔴 Red — Incorrect
- ⚪ Grey — Not Mentioned

**Learning Report**
Review corrections, source evidence, and areas to improve.
