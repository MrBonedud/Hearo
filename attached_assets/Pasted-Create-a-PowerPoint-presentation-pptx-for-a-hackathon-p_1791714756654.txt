THE 10 MISTAKES TO AVOID (build the deck around these)
1. Don't pitch the idea, pitch the product. Show what was built (screenshots of the real app), not a vision.
2. Don't open with "Hi, we are team...". Open with the user's pain. The team comes last (slide 07).
3. No walls of text. One idea per slide, a headline of at most ~8 words, and at most 2-3 short supporting lines. The speaker says it; the slide doesn't spell it out.
4. No live demo without a backup. Slide 04 is a recorded video. Add a placeholder for the embedded video and a fallback screenshot.
5. Don't start the demo at the login screen. The demo slide should jump straight to the moment the AI catches a wrong claim, and add a note saying so.
6. Never say "we have no competitors". The market slide must name real alternatives (WhatsApp study groups, paper notes, flashcard apps, ChatGPT, NotebookLM) and say why Hearo is different.
7. No made-up numbers. Every statistic must have a source on the slide. If I haven't given a source, use [CITE SOURCE: statistic] in red brackets instead of inventing a figure.
8. Don't use "AI" as a buzzword. Show exactly what the AI does (listens, extracts claims, checks them against the course material, flags errors) and why that matters to the learner.
9. Don't go over time. Make sure the speaker notes add up to 180 seconds or less.
10. Don't end without an ask. Slide 08 closes with ONE clear request, then stops. No "thank you" or "any questions?" slide.
