name: Hearo feedback experience
description: Approved Duolingo-inspired feedback without interrupting the learner or treating uncertainty as error.
---

Hearo may use supportive Duolingo-inspired correct/correction cards, but must retain its silent-listening learning flow.

**Why:** The team approved green supported feedback, yellow partial understanding, gentle red source-backed corrections, grey uncertainty, and trying again without forced interruption.

**How to apply:** Keep learner quotations distinct from exact reference evidence. Never treat skipped or uncertain concepts as wrong, and do not add hearts, punitive penalties, forced Continue steps, or spoken AI responses merely to resemble a game.

Optional short feedback tones are compatible with silent-by-default Hearo; spoken answers are not.

**Why:** The team approved experimenting with a small sound after agreeing to optional sounds off by default, a gentle success chime, a soft correction tap, and no sounds for partial/uncertain/skipped concepts.

**How to apply:** Require opt-in, suppress repeated unchanged verdict notifications, avoid alert bursts, and never replay old/restored results as if they were newly checked.

The concept map must read as a connected diagram, not a numbered concept checklist.

**Why:** The team rejected the numbered-card and separate-connections presentation and selected the original Hearo reference showing a transcript beside a dominant branching green/red/grey map.

**How to apply:** Keep relationships visible and colour the whole concept node. Present the source document as session context rather than a catalogue of preset subjects. Relationships must come from the approved source model, never be invented merely to fill the diagram. Partial understanding must be yellow, not the same grey as omissions; low-confidence uncertainty and unchecked concepts remain grey.
